Animal cell structures & functions · Grade 8 · 45 minutes
Every learner explains three relationships and justifies one consequence.

Lens Option and barrier Goal and evidence Resources and decision
Engagement E1: Preview the goal and invite a prediction: “What could happen if one cell part stopped working?” Counters passive copying. Learners begin a causal question; initial ideas are ungraded. All complete the same final evidence. Board and a printed question. Use.
Engagement E2: Choose quiet individual rehearsal or a brief partner explanation before an individual response. Counters first-attempt pressure. Both rehearse a structure → function → consequence explanation. Collaboration cannot substitute for individual evidence. Paper; 4-minute timer; no forced speaking. Use.
Representation R1: Keep a short, readable three-structure reference available, with a simple grayscale diagram and equivalent text. Counters transient/dense information and vocabulary load. Each learner can revisit accurate facts, then explains relationships and constructs their own consequence chain. One accessible handout per learner; enlarged or digital text where available. Use.
Representation R2: Model a causal chain with an everyday water-filter example; retain the written chain while explaining it aloud. Counters an invisible reasoning process. Models the form of causal explanation without giving an assessed cell answer. Explicitly state that a filter is an analogy, not a cell. Board/text transcript; 3 minutes. Use.
Action and expression A1: Offer a blank organizer: structure → normal role → why that role matters; separate failure → disrupted function → consequence. Counters blank-page planning load. Prompts organize evidence without supplying the biological links or a completed prediction. Printed organizer or plain text headings. Use; optional scaffold.
Action and expression A2: Choose paragraph, labeled map with explanatory sentences, or an optional short recording where a device and quiet space already exist. Counters a single response mode. Every mode includes all three relationships and one justified consequence; labels or attractive drawings alone are insufficient. Paper route guaranteed; recording only after access check. Use paper choices now; defer recording.
