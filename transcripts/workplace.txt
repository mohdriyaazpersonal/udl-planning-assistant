Recommended starter set

For this 15-minute lesson, I would avoid deploying all six supports. A feasible starter combination is Representation 1 + Engagement 1 + Action/Expression 1: a revisit-able policy card, private scratch rehearsal, and the three-part planning strip. Together they address the three validated barriers with very little facilitator management. The policy card should remain available through final writing because remembering wording from a one-time oral presentation is not the intended construct.

The planning strip should contain prompts, not sentence starters that effectively compose the response. In particular, it should not tell participants what outcome to promise. The scenario does not establish that the parcel is confirmed lost, and an immediate refund cannot simply be promised because refunds require supervisor approval.

Feasible 15-minute schedule
Time	Activity
0:00–1:30	Facilitator states the goal and fictional complaint; distributes policy card/planning sheet. Learners can read the policy themselves while it is briefly introduced.
1:30–3:30	Policy orientation: individuals locate the four constraints on the card; facilitator clarifies the task but does not construct an answer.
3:30–5:00	Private rehearsal: jot a possible acknowledgement plus the policy point/allowed next step.
5:00–6:30	Plan: use the three boxes—Acknowledge / Explain / Next step.
6:30–12:30	Independent final writing: each person writes the complete professional email. Policy card remains available.
12:30–14:00	Independent check: verify all three content requirements, professional organization, and that no refund, replacement, or delivery date has been promised beyond the policy.
14:00–15:00	Submit final email; facilitator closes by restating the policy decision logic without treating rehearsal notes as assessment evidence.
