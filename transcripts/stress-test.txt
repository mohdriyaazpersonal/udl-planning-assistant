A better approach is to vary access, planning, and rehearsal without supplying the performance itself.

Feasible 15-minute redesign

0–2 min — Accessible policy reference. Give each learner a small printed policy card that remains available throughout: “1. Check shipment status first. 2. Replacement only if shipment is confirmed lost. 3. Refunds require supervisor approval.” This directly addresses the transient-information barrier without turning memory into part of the assessment.

2–5 min — Low-stakes planning/rehearsal. Provide a blank planning frame such as: Acknowledge the problem → Explain what the policy requires → Offer an allowed next step. Learners jot keywords or privately rehearse what they intend to say. Do not provide completed sentences.
